Hi everyone\! We're working on Heritage, a exploration and puzzle game where players explore an abandoned mansion and uncover its forgotten stories through damaged film reels\.

One of our main ideas is allowing players to enter memories recorded on film, explore the past, and piece together missing parts of the story\.

Recently, we've been focusing on designing the core mechanics and how players interact with objects and memories\. One challenge we've discovered is making these interactions feel intuitive while keeping the puzzles interesting\.

We're still experimenting with different ideas, and we're excited to see how they develop\!

Question for other studios: What would make our film reel mechanic more fun and engaging?
